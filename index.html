Analysis Complete - 3 Critical Flags
Section 8.2 - Uncapped Liability (HIGH RISK)
Non-standard under US commercial practice. Unlimited exposure. Recommend: mutual cap at 12x monthly fees. Delaware courts will enforce this clause against your client.
Section 12.1 - Unilateral Indemnification (EU NON-COMPLIANT)
One-way indemnity violates EU Directive 2019/770 on B2B digital services. Bilateral clause required. Unenforceable in 22 of 27 EU member states as drafted.
Section 15.4 - Governing Law Conflict (JURISDICTION RISK)
Delaware law designated, but counterparty is an EU entity. Choice-of-law clause likely unenforceable under Rome I Regulation. Recommend neutral arbitration clause.
